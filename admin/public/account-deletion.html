Delete your account

An account is optional in Nepaliko Radio — the live stream, news and song requests all work without one. If you created an account and want it removed, here is how.

From the app

  1. Open Nepaliko Radio and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. You may be asked for your password if you signed in a while ago.

The account is deleted straight away. Nothing is queued.

By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email nepalikoradioapp@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, asking us to delete your account. We action these within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in credentials
  • Your name, email address, phone number, postal address and date of birth
  • The date you joined and when you were last seen

What is kept, and for how long

Song requests and messages you sent to the station are stored separately from your account and are not linked to your name once it is deleted. We keep them for up to 12 months so the presenters can look back over what was asked for, then they are removed.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. You are welcome to create a new one at any time.
