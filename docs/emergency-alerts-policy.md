# Emergency alerts — station policy

Agree this with the station **before** anyone is given access to the `alerts`
channel in the admin panel.

The `alerts` channel makes a sound, shows as a heads-up banner, and breaks
through Do Not Disturb on every listener's phone. It reaches everyone within
seconds and **cannot be recalled**. Every send is recorded against the account
that sent it.

## The rules

**1. Relay official sources only.**
The app is not a source of hazard information. It repeats what an authority has
already published:

- **NDRRMA** — National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority
- **DHM** — Department of Hydrology and Meteorology (floods, weather)
- **National Seismological Centre** (earthquakes)

If no official body has said it, it does not go out on `alerts`.

**2. Name the source in the message.**
"NDRRMA: flood warning for the Koshi basin" is something a listener can verify.
"Flood warning!" is a rumour with our logo on it.

**3. Never speculate.**
No casualty figures before an authority publishes them. No aftershock
predictions — nobody can make them. No estimates of how bad something will get.

**4. Correct publicly and immediately.**
If something wrong goes out, send a correction to the same channel straight
away. A wrong alert that is never corrected is worse than the original mistake,
because the next one will not be believed.

**5. Keep the channel rare.**
Never use `alerts` for a promotion, a schedule change, or a new show. Those go
to **Station announcements**. If `alerts` starts carrying routine traffic,
people will turn it off — and it will be silent on the day it matters.

## Which channel to use

| Situation | Channel |
| --- | --- |
| NDRRMA / DHM / Seismological Centre advisory | **Emergency alerts** |
| Evacuation or safety instruction from authorities | **Emergency alerts** |
| We are going live, schedule change, station news | **Station announcements** |
| A new article was published | **New posts** |
| Anything promotional | **Station announcements**, or nothing |

## Practical limits

- **10 sends per hour per person.** Deliberate: it bounds the damage from a
  stuck button or a panicking sender.
- **Title 65 characters, message 240.** Longer text is truncated on the lock
  screen — and in an alert the part that gets cut is usually the instruction.
- **A 30-second cooldown** after each send.
- **Typing `SEND`** is required to confirm an emergency alert. Do not develop a
  habit of typing it before reading the message back.

## Before sending an alert

1. Read the official advisory again, in full.
2. Check **Recently sent** — has a colleague already sent this?
3. Read your message back once, out loud.
4. Confirm the channel selector says **Emergency alerts** and you meant it.

## In the app

The About screen carries this line, and it should stay there:

> Emergency alerts relay official government advisories. Always follow
> instructions from local authorities.

Listeners can turn `alerts` off in Settings. That is deliberate — it is their
phone. The setting warns them plainly what they are turning off.
