# Privacy Policy — Nepaliko Radio

**Last updated: 3 August 2026**

Nepaliko Radio ("we", "the station") operates the Nepaliko Radio mobile app. This
policy explains what the app collects and why.

## The short version

The app has no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email
in order to listen. We do not sell data, and we do not use advertising trackers.

## What we collect

### Nothing, if you only listen

Playing the radio requires no personal information. The app downloads the
station's settings, schedule and news posts, and streams audio. None of that
identifies you.

### If you send a message to the studio

The Connect screen lets you send a song request, shout-out or feedback. When you
do, we store:

- the **name** you type,
- the **message** you type,
- an optional **phone number or email**, only if you choose to provide one,
- the time you sent it,
- a random identifier generated on your device.

That random identifier is not your device ID, advertising ID or phone number. It
is a random value the app creates once and stores locally, used only to stop one
device from flooding the studio with messages.

Only station staff can read these messages. They are never shown publicly in the
app. If you include contact details, staff may use them to reach you about your
message.

### If you turn on notifications

If you enable notifications, we store a push token for your device together with
which categories you opted into. The token lets us deliver a notification; it does
not tell us who you are. Turning notifications off in Settings stops them.

### Crash reports

If crash reporting is enabled in a release, we collect technical diagnostics when
the app crashes — device model, OS version and the error. These reports are
configured to exclude personal information, and they never include the contents
of messages you send.

## What we never collect

- Your location
- Your contacts, photos or files
- Microphone audio. The app plays audio; it does not record. It requests no
  microphone permission.
- Advertising identifiers

## Where data goes

Content and messages are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Storage and
Cloud Messaging), operated by Google. Audio is streamed from the station's own
server. Push notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and the
platform notification services (Apple Push Notification service and Firebase
Cloud Messaging).

## How long we keep it

Messages to the studio are kept until station staff delete them. Push tokens are
kept while notifications remain enabled.

## Your choices

- **Notifications:** turn them off any time in the app's Settings screen.
- **Messages:** sending one is entirely optional.
- **Deletion:** to have a message you sent removed, contact us at the address
  below. Because the app has no accounts, please include enough detail for us to
  find it.

## Children

The app is a general-audience radio app. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children.
